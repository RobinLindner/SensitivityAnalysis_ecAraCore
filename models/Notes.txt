The newly generated tGEM model at 20 degrees differs from the one that Yannick generated only in the following points:

- They contain a field TRANS_ID: {'Im_H2O_REV'}
- The upper bound of a water sink reaction is set to 1000 (according to comments to 'simulate transpiration')
- The upper bound of the protein pool exchange is 0.2 instead of 0.078, because it was adapted to the temperature by using the 'proteinModel' function in the model

In my opinion, these differences are find to keep, because the simulateTempEffects script by Philipps gives good reasons for adapting these values.
